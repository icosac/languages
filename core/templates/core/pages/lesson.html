Definite Articles

German uses Der, Die, and Das. Learn article and noun together for stronger recall.

Der

Masculine

Der Mann (The man)

Die

Feminine

Die Frau (The woman)

Das

Neuter

Das Kind (The child)

lightbulb

Mindful Tip

Always learn the article together with the noun. Do not isolate vocabulary from its article.

Study Time

~12 minutes
